We collect information required to provide social media automation and lead analytics services:

Your data is strictly utilized to:

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is only processed through official social platform APIs (Meta Graph API, TikTok API, LinkedIn API) and encrypted infrastructure providers (Supabase, Upstash) strictly for service operation.

Authentication tokens and operational logs are stored securely with end-to-end encryption. Users may revoke access at any time via their respective social account settings (e.g., Facebook Business Integrations, TikTok Security Settings) or request full account data deletion by contacting us.
